01

Touch

Shown at an exhibition. About 80 people tried it.

Touch the inside of the petals and they close.

Copper pads inside the petals sense contact through an MPR121 capacitive sensor. An Arduino sketch turns that into motion, and the gears close the petals gradually.

  1. TouchCopper pads inside the petals.
  2. MPR121A sensor that detects the contact.
  3. ArduinoDecides how far and how fast to move.
  4. MotorEased, so the closing is gradual.
  5. GearsPrinted and laser-cut parts carry it on.
  6. Petals closeThe plant shuts around your hand.
02

Play

Played by about 10 people in a class setting.

Win the game and the plant opens.

In a Quest 3 / Unity game you defeat all 20 monsters. When the “unlock fly” meter reaches 100%, Unity sends a command and the physical sculpture opens its petals.

  1. 20 monstersDefeat them all.
  2. Unlock 100%The game decides it has been won.
  3. UnityOn the Quest 3, sends the command.
  4. SerialA laptop passes it to the Arduino.
  5. ArduinoStarts the eased sweep.
  6. Petals openIn the gallery, in real time.
03

Puppet

Ongoing exploration.

Lift the object and the plant follows.

Pick up a virtual box and the sculpture opens. Drop it and the petals close. The sculpture was re-posed for this stage, with paper petals in place of the originals.

Full overview video: coming soon.

  1. Grab / dropLift the virtual box, or let go.
  2. Unity eventFires on pick-up and release.
  3. SerialPassed on through the laptop.
  4. ArduinoRuns the eased sweep.
  5. ServoTurns 0° to 180° and back.
  6. Open / closeLifted opens, dropped closes.
